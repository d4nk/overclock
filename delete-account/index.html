OVERCLOCK

Delete your account

Overclock is made by Torre Matrix LLC. You can delete your Overclock account and its data at any time.

Option 1: In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Overclock and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete My Account.
  4. Confirm. Your account is deleted right away.

Want a copy first? You can export your data from Profile before deleting.

Option 2: By email

If you can't access the app, email privacy@torrematrix.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my Overclock account". We'll confirm the request and delete the account.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in email
  • Your profile: username, display name and avatar photo
  • Workout history, sets, personal records, ranks and Grit Points
  • Bodyweight and any heart-rate data
  • Friends, parties, custom exercises, notes and settings
  • Training-location preferences

What may be kept

  • Limited purchase records for REDLINE (transaction IDs) that we or the app stores must keep for tax and legal reasons. No card details are ever stored by us.
  • Copies in encrypted backups, which are purged on a rolling schedule.

Deleting your account does not cancel or refund purchases made through the App Store or Google Play; those are handled by the store.

More detail is in our Privacy Policy. Questions: privacy@torrematrix.com
