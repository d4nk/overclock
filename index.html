// A look inside

// What you get — free

▲ CLIMB

Seven tracks — Chest, Back, Shoulders, Arms, Legs, Core, Conditioning — roll into one overall rank. A balance gate means no skipping leg day to climb.

⚖ FAIR

Scored by DOTS, not raw weight. A 130-lb lifter and a 230-lb lifter compete on even ground — and cardio counts too.

◎ COACH

Climb Coach spots your weakest track and tells you what to train next so your overall rank keeps moving.

✚ INJURY GUARD

Working around something that hurts? Flag it and get smart exercise swaps that skip it — still free, still earning.

⚔ SQUAD UP

Add friends with your code, form a party, and race each other on a weekly party leaderboard.

◆ SEASONS

Seasons soft-reset the ladder for a fresh climb. Earn season badges, rank emblems, and a trophy case that shows it.
