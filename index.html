// What you get

▲ CLIMB

Seven tracks — Arms, Legs, Core, Chest, Back, Shoulders, Conditioning — roll into one overall rank. No skipping leg day to climb.

⚖ FAIR

Scored by DOTS, not raw weight. A 130-lb lifter and a 230-lb lifter compete on even ground.

⚔ COMPETE

Party up with friends and climb your gym's leaderboard. Seasons reset the ladder so there's always a fresh climb.

◆ EARN

Badges, achievements, and REDLINE flair as you level up. Your hologram body-map lights up as each muscle group ranks.
